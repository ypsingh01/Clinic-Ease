1.4 Non-Functional Requirements
Category Requirement
Performance Handle 100+ bookings/week comfortably; slot queries return in <500ms
Reliability No double-booking under concurrent requests (transactional integrity)
Usability Mobile-responsive; booking flow completable in under 3 minutes
Security Passwords hashed (berypt); JWT-based auth; role-based access control (RBAC);
rate limiting + CAPTCHA on public booking/registration endpoints to prevent
bot/fake token abuse
Availability System usable during clinic hours without manual intervention
Data Privacy Patient medical/contact data access restricted by role
Maintainability Modular codebase (routes/controllers/services separated) for future feature
additions
Transparency ~~ ETAs are always communicated as estimates/windows, never guarantees, to
manage patient expectations
