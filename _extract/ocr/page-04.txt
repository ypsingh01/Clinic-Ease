2.2 Core Database Entities (Conceptual)

« users (id, name, email, phone, password_hash, role[patient/doctor/admin])

« doctors (id, user_id FK, specialty, bio, photo_url, consultation_fee,
avg_consultation_minutes — rolling average, updated after each completed
appointment)

« doctor_availability (id, doctor_id FK, day_of_week, start_time, end_time,
hourly_capacity — replaces fixed slot_duration_minutes; capacity derived from
avg_consultation_minutes but doctor-overridable)

+ doctor_leaves (id, doctor_id FK, date, reason)

« appointments (id, patient_id FK, doctor_id FK, booked_for_dependent_id — nullable
FK to dependents, hour_block_start, queue_position — token number within the
hour block, estimated_time — recalculated dynamically, actual_start_time,
actual_end_time, status[held/booked/in_progress/completed/cancelled/no_show],
payment_id FK, created_by_admin flag)

« payments (id, appointment _id FK, razorpay_order_id, razorpay_payment_id, amount,
status)

« notifications (id, user_id FK, type[in_app/whatsapp], message, status, sent_at)

« queue_delay_offsets (id, doctor_id FK, date, offset_minutes, reason) — tracks manual
"running late" adjustments applied to a day's queue

« waitlist (id, patient_id FK, doctor_id FK, date, hour_block_start, position,
status[waiting/notified/claimed/expired], created_at)

« dependents (id, patient_user_id FK, name, relation, date_of_birth, gender)

« symptom _specialty_map (id, symptom_keyword, specialty) — small curated lookup
table; no ML needed given a fixed roster of 5-6 doctors

(Full schema with constraints/indexes to be detailed in a dedicated schema doc — happy to
build that next.)
